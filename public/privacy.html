Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

In short

  • This is a personal portfolio. It has no accounts or forms, and it doesn’t sell or share your information.
  • It uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which parts of the site are viewed. Visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked first.
  • Browser “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals are honored.
  • Questions: samanthaponce.dev@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible

This website, samanthaponce.dev, is Samantha’s personal portfolio. Samantha is responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here and can be reached at samanthaponce.dev@gmail.com.

2. Information collected

Usage information (analytics). If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics collects information about how the site is used, such as:

  • the pages and sections viewed, scrolling, and use of the site’s buttons, links and settings (for example the menu, the theme switch, display settings, the résumé and contact links, and the 3D scene);
  • the website that referred you;
  • device, browser and operating system information;
  • your approximate location (country, region and city), derived from your IP address. Google Analytics doesn’t store IP addresses.

Technical information (hosting). The site’s host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the website and protect it from abuse, and to identify your country so the site knows whether to ask for your consent to analytics.

Emails you send. If you email, your email address and message are used only to reply to you.

The site doesn’t collect your name, contact details or anything you type, and it doesn’t use analytics to identify you.

3. Why it’s used, and the legal basis

  • Understanding how the site is used (analytics), to improve it. For visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, this happens only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Delivering and securing the website (hosting), based on the legitimate interest in running a working, secure website.
  • Replying to your emails, based on the legitimate interest in responding to you.

The information isn’t used for advertising or to make automated decisions about you. Google Analytics’ advertising features are turned off.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

  • Analytics cookies. If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics sets cookies that distinguish visits and returning visitors. They expire after up to 2 years.
  • Preferences. The site stores your display settings (theme, text size, contrast and motion) and your analytics choice in your browser. These stay on your device and aren’t shared.

5. Who receives it

Information is shared only with the service providers that run the site, which process it on the site’s behalf:

  • Google (Google Analytics). See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare (hosting and security). See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Your information isn’t sold, or shared for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. International transfers

Google and Cloudflare may process information in the United States and other countries. Where information comes from the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, they rely on recognized safeguards, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. framework) and standard contractual clauses.

7. How long it’s kept

  • Analytics information: up to 14 months.
  • Emails: only as long as needed to respond.
  • Hosting logs: according to Cloudflare’s own retention practices.

8. Your choices

  • Visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland can allow or decline analytics when they first visit.
  • If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, analytics isn’t used.
  • You can turn analytics off, or reset your choice to be asked again, at any time:
  • Turn analytics off in this browser
  • Reset my choice

You can also delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email samanthaponce.dev@gmail.com. Because analytics information isn’t linked to your identity, it may not be possible to find information about you specifically.

If you are in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

This site isn’t directed at children under 13, and doesn’t knowingly collect information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, email samanthaponce.dev@gmail.com.

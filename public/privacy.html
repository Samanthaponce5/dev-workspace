Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This is Samantha’s personal portfolio at samanthaponce.dev. It doesn’t ask you for any information, has no accounts and no forms, and doesn’t sell or share anything about you. The one thing it measures is how the site is used, with Google Analytics, as described below.

What Google Analytics collects

To see how many people visit and which parts of the site they look at, the site uses Google Analytics. It records:

  • which pages and sections are viewed, how far down the page you scroll, and clicks on the résumé, email, LinkedIn and GitHub links;
  • the site that sent you (for example LinkedIn or a search engine);
  • your device type, browser, operating system and screen size;
  • your approximate location (country, region and city), which Google works out from your IP address. Google Analytics doesn’t store IP addresses.

It never collects your name, email address or anything you type. The figures are seen only in aggregate, and they’re kept for up to 14 months. Google’s advertising features (Google signals and ad personalisation) are turned off, so this information isn’t used to show you ads.

Google processes this data on the site’s behalf. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy.

Cookies and similar storage

If analytics runs, Google Analytics sets two cookies on this site:

  • _ga and _ga_HMT3FWC1FW, which tell visits apart and count returning visitors. They last up to 2 years.

The site also keeps two small settings in your browser’s own storage, which never leave your device: your display settings (theme, text size, contrast and motion) and, if you’ve made one, your analytics choice.

Your choices

  • Visitors in the EU, the rest of the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked first. Google Analytics doesn’t load, and no analytics cookies are set, unless you choose Allow.
  • If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the site doesn’t use Google Analytics at all.
  • You can turn analytics off in your browser at any time, or forget the choice you made so that you’re asked again:
  • Turn analytics off in this browser
  • Forget my choice

Turning analytics off stops new data being collected; any analytics cookies already set expire on their own, or you can clear them in your browser’s settings. Google also offers a browser add-on that opts you out of Google Analytics on every site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks. Cloudflare also tells the site which country you’re visiting from, so it knows whether to ask you about cookies first. The site doesn’t store that.

Children

The site isn’t aimed at children and doesn’t knowingly collect information about anyone under 13.

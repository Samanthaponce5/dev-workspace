Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Samantha (“I,” “me,” or “my”) collects, uses and shares information when you visit samanthaponce.dev (the “Site”), my personal portfolio. By using the Site, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information I Collect

The Site does not require you to create an account or submit any forms. I collect the following information:

Usage Information. When analytics is enabled, Google Analytics collects information about your use of the Site, including the pages and sections you view, your interactions with buttons, links and settings, the website that referred you, your device type, browser and operating system, and your approximate location (such as country, region and city), which is derived from your IP address. Google Analytics does not store your IP address.

Technical Information. My hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP address and basic browser information to deliver the Site, protect it against abuse, and determine your country so that the Site can request your consent to analytics where required.

Information You Provide. If you contact me by email, I receive your email address and the contents of your message.

How I Use Your Information

I use the information described above to:

  • understand how visitors use the Site and improve it;
  • operate, maintain and secure the Site; and
  • respond to your messages.

I do not use your information for advertising, and I do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

When analytics is enabled, Google Analytics places cookies on your device to distinguish visitors and sessions. These cookies expire after up to two years. The Site also stores your display preferences (such as theme and text size) and your analytics choice in your browser’s local storage. This information remains on your device and is not shared.

Google Analytics

The Site uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC. Google Analytics’ advertising features, including Google signals and ad personalization, are disabled. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, I process your information on the following legal bases: analytics, only with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time; hosting and security, based on my legitimate interest in operating a secure website; and responding to your messages, based on my legitimate interest in replying to you.

How I Share Your Information

I share information only with service providers that process it on my behalf: Google (analytics) and Cloudflare (hosting and security). See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. I do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

International Data Transfers

Google and Cloudflare may process information in the United States and other countries. Where information is transferred from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, these providers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (including the UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) and standard contractual clauses.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months. Emails are retained only as long as necessary to respond to you. Hosting logs are retained in accordance with Cloudflare’s practices.

Your Choices

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Site asks for your consent before enabling analytics. Wherever you are located, you may allow or refuse analytics cookies at any time by selecting Cookie settings at the bottom of any page of the Site. If you refuse, analytics stops and its cookies are deleted. You may also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the Site does not use Google Analytics.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, contact me using the details below. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, I may be unable to locate information about you specifically. If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact me at samanthaponce.dev@gmail.com.
